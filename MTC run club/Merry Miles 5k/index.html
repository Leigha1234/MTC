Your December
feel-good 5K.

Merry Miles brings the MTC community together for a festive morning outdoors. Enjoy the run, cheer each other over the finish and head to The Den afterwards for coffee, hot chocolate and cake.

Go festive if you fancy it — Santa hats, Christmas jumpers and comfortable costumes are all welcome. Come dressed to move and ready for the Scottish weather.

The route, capacity and final event arrangements are being confirmed. This page is a preview of the proposed event.

Before you lace up

A few useful
details.

Where does it start?

MTC in Lossiemouth is the proposed event base. The exact start point and route will be shared once confirmed.

Will there be chip timing?

No. The plan is one shared start with simple stopwatch timing. If individual results are recorded, times will be measured from the starting signal.

Can I walk or bring children?

Walking options, age limits and any supervision requirements are still being confirmed against the route and event arrangements. Full participation details will be published before bookings open.

Do I have to wear festive dress?

No — it’s optional. If you dress up, choose something that lets you move comfortably and keeps your vision clear.

What happens in bad weather?

We’ll publish weather, cancellation, postponement and refund arrangements before taking entries. Any race-day updates will be sent to registered participants.

Can I train with MTC beforehand?

The proposed MTC Run Club launch is Sunday 25 October at 10am, with seven Sunday sessions before race day. It will be free for now, with a welcoming run/walk option, finishing at The Den for optional coffee and cake at normal café prices.

When can I book?

Bookings are not open yet. The form below is a demo only. Entry deadlines and the medal ordering cutoff will be published when arrangements are confirmed.
