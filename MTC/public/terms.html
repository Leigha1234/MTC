Terms & Conditions

Welcome to The Pack. These Terms & Conditions govern your membership and use of our facility in Lossiemouth. By training with us, you agree to follow the standards set out below.

1. Membership & Booking

  • Booking Policy: All class attendance must be booked in advance via the TeamUp app.
  • Cancellation Window: Members must cancel their spot at least 2 hours before a class starts to allow other members to take the slot.
  • Trial Sessions: New members are entitled to one trial session, which must be booked through the website or reception desk.

2. Health & Safety

Assumption of Risk: Training at Moray Training Club involves high-intensity movements (CrossFit, Hyrox, Powerlifting). Members acknowledge that they participate at their own risk.

Physical Readiness: Members must complete a PAR-Q upon joining. If your health status changes, you must inform a coach immediately.

Beach Sunrise Sessions: Participants in outdoor sessions must follow coach instructions regarding tide safety and environmental conditions.

3. Conduct & "The Pack" Philosophy

Respect: Harassment or disrespectful behavior toward coaches or fellow members will result in immediate membership termination.

Equipment Care: Members are expected to treat the hangar and its equipment with respect—clean your station and put away your weights.

Timeliness: Please arrive at least 5 minutes before your session. Late arrivals may be denied entry for safety reasons.

4. Payments & Personal Training

Membership Fees: All fees are processed via TeamUp. Memberships can be paused or cancelled with 30 days' notice via email.

1:1 PT Sessions: A 24-hour cancellation policy applies to all 1:1 slots; late cancellations will be charged in full.

5. Media & Privacy

Photography: We frequently capture content during classes. By joining, you consent to being featured in promotional material unless you explicitly opt-out in writing.

6. Events & Competitions

For external events like MacTuff or Hyrox, Moray Training Club acts as a training facilitator. Entry fees for these events are the responsibility of the individual member.
