Cookie Policy

To keep the "Pack" website running smoothly and ensure a seamless booking experience, we use cookies. This policy explains how we use them and your choices regarding your data.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences, keep you logged into the TeamUp booking system, and understand how you interact with our training schedule.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience in the following ways:

Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function, such as allowing you to log in to book CrossFit WODs or Hyrox sessions.

Performance Cookies: These help us track which pages are most popular—for example, if members are checking the Beach Sunrise schedule or the Events Calendar.

Functional Cookies: These remember your settings, such as your preferred view of the weekly timetable.

Marketing Cookies: Used to show you relevant updates about upcoming events like Friday Night Lights.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use: TeamUp (membership management), Google Maps (location services), and Social Media (content sharing).

4. Your Choices

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies via your browser settings or our consent banner. Note: Rejecting essential cookies may cause the TeamUp booking integration to fail.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in technology or for legal reasons. Please check back regularly to stay informed.
