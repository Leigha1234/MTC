PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: May 2026

1. DATA COLLECTION

At Moray Training Club, we collect personal information via our booking partner, TeamUp. This includes your name, contact details, and emergency contact information to ensure your safety within the hangar.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your data is used solely for membership management, class bookings, and occasional "Pack" updates regarding gym events. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. HEALTH DATA

Information regarding physical health (PAR-Q) is stored securely to allow our coaches to tailor workouts and ensure safe participation in CrossFit and Hyrox activities.
