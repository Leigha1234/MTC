FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need to be fit to start?

Absolutely not. Every movement in the Hangar is scalable. Whether you're a pro athlete or haven't exercised in years, we adapt the workout to your current level.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable gym gear, a water bottle, and a positive attitude. We have all the specialized equipment you need. Check your Welcome Pack for more details!

How do I book classes?

We use the TeamUp app for all bookings. Once you've selected a plan, you'll get a login to manage your schedule from your phone.

Is there parking at the Hangar?

Yes, we have dedicated parking right outside Unit 7 in the Coulardbank Industrial Estate.
